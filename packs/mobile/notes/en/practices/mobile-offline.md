Define the local source of truth, pending-operation queue, retry limits and conflict policy before implementing sync. Test airplane mode, process restart, expired authentication and duplicate requests. Cache persistence is not a synchronization protocol. Store credentials in platform-appropriate secure facilities rather than ordinary preferences.

[Official reference](https://developer.android.com/topic/architecture/data-layer/offline-first).
