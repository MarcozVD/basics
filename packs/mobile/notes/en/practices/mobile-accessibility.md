Give controls meaningful labels, roles and states. Test focus order, screen readers, text scaling, contrast and touch targets on both platforms. Preserve reduced-motion preferences and provide alternatives to gesture-only actions. Component assertions supplement real device checks; neither a screenshot nor one passing test proves accessibility.

[Official reference](https://reactnative.dev/docs/accessibility).
