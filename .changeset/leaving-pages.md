Leaving a page no longer slows down or rewrites the next one. Leaving the graph while it is still drawing no longer freezes the next page for a moment, whether you leave with a click, the command palette, a `g` shortcut or Back. An Inbox you leave no longer adds its `?p=` selection to the next page's address. Back and Forward keep working when you click through pages quickly.
